Our Story

A personal touch on every build, renovation and repair.

Family owned and growing. Copley General Contracting was founded with one simple idea: treat every project like it's being built for our own family. For over 10 years, Chad and Dede Copley have served homeowners and businesses across the Tampa Bay area with custom builds, major renovations, structural repairs, and remediation work.

From our home base in Saint Petersburg, we've grown into a trusted name in Florida construction while keeping the values that started it all: integrity, craftsmanship, and clear communication on every job, no matter the size.

Tampa Bay Roots

Proud to serve our neighbors.

From historic Saint Petersburg bungalows to brand-new custom homes along the coast, our work is woven into the neighborhoods we live and raise our families in. Copley knows Florida construction very well: its weather, its codes, its character. We build with that knowledge in mind.

Based in Saint Petersburg, we work throughout Pinellas, Hillsborough, and the surrounding Gulf Coast communities. If you're nearby, chances are we've already worked down the street.
